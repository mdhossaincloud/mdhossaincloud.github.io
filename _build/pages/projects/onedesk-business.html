OneDesk Business

A business and accounting platform in development: a modern, premium workspace for running a business day to day, built so the product catalogue can grow without a limit.

BusinessAccountingIn development

Overview

OneDesk Business extends the OneDesk family from personal task tracking to the way a business keeps its books and manages what it sells. It follows the familiar logic of an accounting application, with a more modern and polished interface suited to today’s business platforms.

Direction

  • Accounting-style workflows presented in a clean, premium interface.
  • A product model with no fixed ceiling: the catalogue is designed to hold an unlimited number of products.
  • Built in stages. The first stage integrates two products, which proves the structure before more are added.

Current status

In active development. The first two products are integrated. This page will be extended with the architecture, screenshots and release notes as the platform matures; until then it only describes what has been built.
