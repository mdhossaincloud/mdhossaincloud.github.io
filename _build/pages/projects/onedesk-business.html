OneDesk Business

A business and accounting platform: a modern, premium workspace for running a business day to day, built so the product catalogue can grow without a limit.

BusinessAccountingLive

Overview

OneDesk Business extends the OneDesk family from personal task tracking to the way a business keeps its books and manages what it sells. It follows the familiar logic of an accounting application, with a more modern and polished interface suited to today’s business platforms.

Direction

  • Accounting-style workflows presented in a clean, premium interface.
  • A product model with no fixed ceiling: the catalogue is designed to hold an unlimited number of products.
  • Delivered in stages: two products are integrated, which proves the structure before more are added.
