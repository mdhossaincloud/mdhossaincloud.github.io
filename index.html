The person who keeps the office connected

I am an IT support specialist and systems & network administrator. Since 2018 I have worked inside Bangladeshi manufacturing and group-company environments, where IT has to keep pace with production schedules, regional offices and people who simply need things to work.

At MKR Group I provide L2/L3 support for more than 150 users across four regional offices. My week moves between Microsoft 365 and Active Directory administration, MikroTik routing and site-to-site VPN tunnels, IP CCTV and NVR operations, ISP and vendor escalations, and keeping the asset registers and network documentation accurate.

Before that, at Alif Embroidery Village Ltd, I handled first-line L1/L2 support for 250+ employees, workstation rollouts and network stability across production facilities.

Outside my job I build small Windows desktop tools in Python, and I run Fixible IT, my own technology services and e-commerce venture.
