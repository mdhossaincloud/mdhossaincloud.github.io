Mission Log 001

Multi-site IT operations

 Ongoing · 4 offices

Problem

Four regional offices (Dhaka, Chittagong, Khulna, Payra) needed consistent L2/L3 support, reliable LAN/WAN connectivity and coordinated vendor relationships — without a large centralised IT team.

Approach

Structured incident and request management through ServiceNow. Maintained MikroTik routing, VLANs, Wi-Fi and site-to-site VPN tunnels across sites. Coordinated ISP escalations and equipped new branches with workstations, network devices and IP telephony.

Outcome

150+ users supported across four offices. Average SLA compliance above 98%. Network uptime maintained at 99.9% across multi-site operations.

Mission Log 002

Microsoft 365 & identity management

 Ongoing

Problem

User lifecycle management was manual and inconsistent — leading to access drift, missed offboarding steps and support overhead.

Approach

Administered Microsoft 365 and Outlook (accounts, groups, permissions). Used Active Directory and Group Policy for access rules and workstation settings. Automated provisioning with PowerShell scripts. Documented onboarding and offboarding workflows.

Outcome

Repeatable, auditable account lifecycle. Reduced manual provisioning time and clearer control over permissions and access reviews.

Mission Log 003

IP CCTV & NVR deployment

 Multi-site · 256 cameras

Problem

Multiple locations required reliable video coverage with consistent recording quality and predictable maintenance.

Approach

Deployed and maintained 256 IP cameras and NVRs across six locations. Planned IP allocation and network capacity. Ran preventive maintenance cycles and kept asset registers, topology notes and CCTV infrastructure documentation up to date.

Outcome

Stable recording across all sites. Reduced footage loss through proactive checks. Documented infrastructure that new team members can operate from.
