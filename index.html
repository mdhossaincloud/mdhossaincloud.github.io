Keeping people and systems connected

My IT career began in 2018 on the support desk of a Bangladeshi manufacturer, keeping 250+ people productive on the factory floor and in the office. Since 2020 I have been Senior IT Officer at MKR Group, responsible for users, networks and security systems across four regional offices.

I care about the quiet kind of IT: networks that stay up, accounts that are created and removed correctly, cameras that keep recording, and documentation that lets someone else take over tomorrow.

I founded Fixible IT to bring the same standards to small businesses and homes in Bangladesh, with IT services and an online store for technology products. In my own time I build small Windows tools in Python.

Practical expertise, sensible automation and customer-focused service, applied to real problems.

Inside a business server rack

Scroll to walk through the equipment that keeps an office running, from the internet connection down to the backup power.

  1. U23

    Edge firewall & router

    Connects the office to the internet, filters traffic and builds the encrypted VPN tunnels to other branches.

  2. U22

    Patch panel

    Every cable from desks, cameras and access points ends here, labelled, so any port can be traced in seconds.

  3. U20

    Core switch

    Moves traffic between every wired device and keeps staff, guests and cameras apart in separate VLANs.

  4. U15–U18

    Servers

    File shares, directory services and management tools that need to stay inside the building.

  5. U10–U11

    NVR (video recorder)

    Records every IP camera on its own network and keeps footage for the agreed retention period.

  6. U2–U3

    UPS (backup power)

    Keeps everything running through short power cuts and shuts systems down safely during long ones.

  7. 01001001 01010100

    Underneath it all: ones and zeros

    Every email, file and video frame that passes through the rack is binary data in the end. Keep scrolling and watch it come apart.
