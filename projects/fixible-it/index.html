Fixible IT

My technology services and e-commerce venture in Bangladesh, offering IT support, networking, CCTV, custom PC builds and technology products, with a service-first website to match.

Visit fixibleit.com Project structure

Overview

Fixible IT brings the work I do professionally (IT support, networking and CCTV) to small businesses and individuals, alongside an online shop for IT products, mobile accessories and gadgets. It is my own business and is separate from my employment.

Problem

Customers looking for IT help in Bangladesh often have to go to one place for services and another for hardware. The business needed a website that explains its services clearly, sells products, and still feels trustworthy and easy to use on a phone.

Objective

  • A service-first website, because services are the larger part of the business.
  • A complete shopping journey: browse, select, cart, checkout.
  • Consistent page names and clean URLs.
  • A clear route from every service to a contact or service request.

Architecture

WordPress and WooCommerce provide content, catalogue and orders. A custom child theme on top of Kadence carries the Fixible IT design and custom functionality, so parent-theme updates do not overwrite it. Theme work happens on a local copy first and is tracked in Git.

Services offered

  • Wi-Fi and router set-up, office network and Wi-Fi
  • VLAN, VPN and MikroTik configuration
  • Email and Microsoft 365, Active Directory and policies
  • Servers and backups, monthly IT support, on-site engineering
  • CCTV for homes, CCTV and access set-up
  • PC and laptop repair, custom PC builds

The online store covers PC components, networking, CCTV and surveillance, office and productivity equipment, and desktop accessories.

Implementation

  • Custom Fixible IT child theme on the Kadence parent theme.
  • WooCommerce catalogue organised by product category.
  • Service-first home page and service pages, each leading to a contact or service request.
  • Local development and full QA before changes go live, on cPanel hosting.

Status and roadmap
